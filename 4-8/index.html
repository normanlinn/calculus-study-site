Newton's Method

Find a root by sliding down tangent lines: one formula, repeated.

0 / 5 problems mastered

Not on Quiz 5, but it's on Midterm 3 and the final. Learn it after Quiz 5.

Start with a guess x1x_1x1​. Draw the tangent line at (x1,f(x1))(x_1,f(x_1))(x1​,f(x1​)), see where it hits the x-axis, and call that x2x_2x2​. Repeat. Each new guess is usually much closer to the root.

xn+1=xn−f(xn)f′(xn)x_{n+1}=x_n-\frac{f(x_n)}{f'(x_n)}xn+1​=xn​−f′(xn​)f(xn​)​

Where it comes from: the tangent line y=f(xn)+f′(xn)(x−xn)y=f(x_n)+f'(x_n)(x-x_n)y=f(xn​)+f′(xn​)(x−xn​) set to y=0y=0y=0.

Solving an equation

Move everything to one side first: “solve cos⁡x=x\cos x=xcosx=x” becomes f(x)=cos⁡x−x=0f(x)=\cos x-x=0f(x)=cosx−x=0.

Approximating a root like 303\sqrt[3]{30}330​

Turn it into an equation: x3=30⇒f(x)=x3−30x^3=30\Rightarrow f(x)=x^3-30x3=30⇒f(x)=x3−30.

When it fails

If f′(xn)=0f'(x_n)=0f′(xn​)=0 the tangent is flat and never hits the axis. A bad first guess can also jump to a different root or wander off.

Calculator

Use radians for trig, and keep at least 6 decimals between steps. Round only the final answer.

Traps
