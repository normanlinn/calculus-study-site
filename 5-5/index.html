The Substitution Rule

u-substitution: the chain rule backwards. Spot the inside function and its derivative.

0 / 9 problems mastered
  1. Pick uuu = the inside function (under a power, root, exponent, or in a denominator).
  2. Find du=u′ dxdu=u'\,dxdu=u′dx. Its pieces should already be in the integral (up to a constant).
  3. Rewrite everything in uuu and integrate.
  4. Put xxx back. For a definite integral, change the limits to uuu-values instead.

Traps

Leaving x in the u-integral

Everything must turn into uuu, including dxdxdx. If an xxx is left over, pick a different uuu.

Old limits after substituting

For definite integrals, either change the limits to uuu-values or substitute xxx back. Don't mix them.
