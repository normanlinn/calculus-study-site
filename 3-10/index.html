Linear Approximations and Differentials

Use the tangent line to estimate values and measurement errors.

0 / 15 problems mastered

Zoom in on a smooth curve and it looks like its tangent line. So near x=ax=ax=a we can use the tangent line, which is easy to compute, in place of the function, which may be hard to compute.

Linearization
L(x)=f(a)+f′(a)(x−a)L(x)=f(a)+f'(a)(x-a)L(x)=f(a)+f′(a)(x−a)

It's the tangent line at aaa, written as a function.

Near a, the tangent line hugs the curve, so L(x) is a great estimate. Move x farther away and the gap grows. On the graph, dy is the rise along the tangent and Δy is the real rise along the curve.
