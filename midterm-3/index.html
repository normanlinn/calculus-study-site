Midterm 3 study plan

Midterm 3 is Tue, Dec 1. It most likely covers everything since Midterm 2: §4.3–4.9 and Ch. 5. A week-long plan, the sections, and a practice exam.

Day by day to Dec 1

  1. Tue Nov 24 · warm-up. Do the 16 algebra & trig warm-up problems. They are inside every Ch. 4–5 problem.
  2. Wed Nov 25 · 4.3, 4.4, 4.5. Redo every problem you didn't tick on Quiz 5. Sign maps until they are automatic.
  3. Thu Nov 26 (Thanksgiving) · 4.7, 4.8, 4.9. Light day: 1–2 hours.
  4. Fri Nov 27 · 5.1, 5.2, 5.3. Riemann sums by hand, the limit-of-sums integral, both parts of the FTC.
  5. Sat Nov 28 · 5.4, 5.5. Substitution is the biggest skill: do all 9 problems in 5.5.
  6. Sun Nov 29 · practice exam. 2 hours, timed, closed notes. Grade it, then redo every miss with the debugger.
  7. Mon Nov 30 · review. Read the Quiz 5 and Midterm 3 & final cheat sheets, do the quick quiz on Midterm 3, redo anything still shaky. Sleep early.
  8. Tue Dec 1 · Midterm 3. 15 minutes before: the 8 things to memorize below.

Quiz 6 (take-home) is also due Dec 1. Its problems are the same skills, so it doubles as practice.
