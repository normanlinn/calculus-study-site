A nudge travels down the chain

Lab 6 · y = sin(x²)
Δu / Δx ≈ du/dx = 2x–
Δy / Δu ≈ dy/du = cos u–
product of the two–
exact dy/dx = 2x·cos(x²)–

The inner function u = x² stretches the nudge by a factor of 2x. The outer function y = sin u stretches it again by cos u. Total stretch = the product. That is the chain rule.

How to spot inside vs. outside

Ask: if I plugged in a number, what would I compute last? That's the outer function.

  • x3+1\sqrt{x^3+1}x3+1​: last step is the square root. Outer u\sqrt uu​, inner u=x3+1u=x^3+1u=x3+1.
  • sin⁡2x=(sin⁡x)2\sin^2x=(\sin x)^2sin2x=(sinx)2: last step is squaring. Outer u2u^2u2, inner sin⁡x\sin xsinx.
  • sin⁡(x2)\sin(x^2)sin(x2): last step is sin. Outer sin⁡u\sin usinu, inner x2x^2x2.
  • Long chains peel like an onion: (f(g(h(x))))′=f′(g(h(x)))⋅g′(h(x))⋅h′(x)\big(f(g(h(x)))\big)'=f'(g(h(x)))\cdot g'(h(x))\cdot h'(x)(f(g(h(x))))′=f′(g(h(x)))⋅g′(h(x))⋅h′(x).
