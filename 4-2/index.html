If fff is continuous on [a,b][a,b][a,b], differentiable on (a,b)(a,b)(a,b), and f(a)=f(b)f(a)=f(b)f(a)=f(b), then there is a ccc in (a,b)(a,b)(a,b) with f′(c)=0f'(c)=0f′(c)=0.

Mean Value Theorem

If fff is continuous on [a,b][a,b][a,b] and differentiable on (a,b)(a,b)(a,b), there is a ccc in (a,b)(a,b)(a,b) with

f′(c)=f(b)−f(a)b−a.f'(c)=\frac{f(b)-f(a)}{b-a}.f′(c)=b−af(b)−f(a)​.
