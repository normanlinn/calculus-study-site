Hyperbolic Functions

sinh, cosh and tanh: the e-based cousins of sin, cos and tan.

0 / 14 problems mastered

Hyperbolic functions are built from exe^xex and e−xe^{-x}e−x. They behave a lot like trig functions, with a few sign flips.

Definitions
sinh⁡x=ex−e−x2cosh⁡x=ex+e−x2\sinh x=\frac{e^x-e^{-x}}{2}\qquad\cosh x=\frac{e^x+e^{-x}}{2}sinhx=2ex−e−x​coshx=2ex+e−x​

tanh⁡x=sinh⁡xcosh⁡x\tanh x=\frac{\sinh x}{\cosh x}tanhx=coshxsinhx​, sech⁡x=1cosh⁡x\operatorname{sech}x=\frac{1}{\cosh x}sechx=coshx1​, csch⁡x=1sinh⁡x\operatorname{csch}x=\frac1{\sinh x}cschx=sinhx1​, coth⁡x=cosh⁡xsinh⁡x\coth x=\frac{\cosh x}{\sinh x}cothx=sinhxcoshx​

Key identity
cosh⁡2x−sinh⁡2x=1\cosh^2x-\sinh^2x=1cosh2x−sinh2x=1

Minus, unlike cos⁡2+sin⁡2=1\cos^2+\sin^2=1cos2+sin2=1. Divide by cosh⁡2\cosh^2cosh2: 1−tanh⁡2x=sech⁡2x1-\tanh^2x=\operatorname{sech}^2x1−tanh2x=sech2x.

Symmetry & values

sinh⁡\sinhsinh is odd, cosh⁡\coshcosh is even. sinh⁡0=0\sinh0=0sinh0=0, cosh⁡0=1\cosh0=1cosh0=1. cosh⁡x+sinh⁡x=ex\cosh x+\sinh x=e^xcoshx+sinhx=ex.

cosh and sinh live on a hyperbola

Lab · slide t
cosh t = (eᵗ + e⁻ᵗ)/2–
sinh t = (eᵗ − e⁻ᵗ)/2–
cosh² t − sinh² t–

Just as (cos t, sin t) is always on the circle x² + y² = 1, the point (cosh t, sinh t) is always on the hyperbola x² − y² = 1. That's where the name comes from, and why the identity has a minus sign.

Derivatives

sinh and cosh
(sinh⁡x)′=cosh⁡x(cosh⁡x)′=sinh⁡x(\sinh x)'=\cosh x\qquad(\cosh x)'=\sinh x(sinhx)′=coshx(coshx)′=sinhx

No minus sign on cosh, unlike (cos⁡x)′=−sin⁡x(\cos x)'=-\sin x(cosx)′=−sinx.

tanh and coth
(tanh⁡x)′=sech⁡2x(coth⁡x)′=−csch⁡2x(\tanh x)'=\operatorname{sech}^2x\qquad(\coth x)'=-\operatorname{csch}^2x(tanhx)′=sech2x(cothx)′=−csch2x
sech and csch
(sech⁡x)′=−sech⁡xtanh⁡x(csch⁡x)′=−csch⁡xcoth⁡x(\operatorname{sech}x)'=-\operatorname{sech}x\tanh x\qquad(\operatorname{csch}x)'=-\operatorname{csch}x\coth x(sechx)′=−sechxtanhx(cschx)′=−cschxcothx
Inverses
(sinh⁡−1x)′=11+x2, (cosh⁡−1x)′=1x2−1, (tanh⁡−1x)′=11−x2(\sinh^{-1}x)'=\frac{1}{\sqrt{1+x^2}},\ (\cosh^{-1}x)'=\frac{1}{\sqrt{x^2-1}},\ (\tanh^{-1}x)'=\frac{1}{1-x^2}(sinh−1x)′=1+x2​1​, (cosh−1x)′=x2−1​1​, (tanh−1x)′=1−x21​

Memory trick: it's the trig table with one change: cosh's derivative has no minus. sech, csch and coth keep their minus signs, just like sec, csc and cot.

Traps

A minus on cosh′

(cosh⁡x)′=sinh⁡x(\cosh x)'=\sinh x(coshx)′=sinhx, with no minus. That's the opposite of cos.

Wrong identity sign

cosh⁡2x−sinh⁡2x=1\cosh^2x-\sinh^2x=1cosh2x−sinh2x=1. The trig version has a plus.

Treating sinh as sin

sinh⁡x\sinh xsinhx is not periodic and not bounded: sinh⁡x→∞\sinh x\to\inftysinhx→∞ as x→∞x\to\inftyx→∞.
