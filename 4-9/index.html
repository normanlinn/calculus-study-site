Antiderivatives

Run the derivative rules backwards, add +C, and use initial conditions to find C.

Traps

Forgetting \(+C\)

“Most general antiderivative” without +C+C+C loses marks.

Antiderivative of a product or quotient

There's no product rule backwards. Expand or split first (#4).

\(\sin\) / \(\cos\) signs

∫sin⁡x=−cos⁡x\int\sin x=-\cos x∫sinx=−cosx and ∫cos⁡x=+sin⁡x\int\cos x=+\sin x∫cosx=+sinx. Differentiate your answer to check.
