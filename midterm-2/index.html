Exam-day strategy

First pass

Do every problem you're sure of first. Mark the rest and come back. Don't spend 15 minutes stuck on one.

Show the setup

Write the rule you're using (“chain rule: outside′·inside′”). Partial credit lives in the setup.

Related rates

Picture → equation → differentiate in ttt → plug in. Units in the answer.

Max/min

Always make the table: critical numbers inside the interval plus both endpoints.

Check

Plug a simple xxx into your derivative and see if the slope makes sense. Check every sign when you distribute a minus.

No calculator

Leave answers exact (89π\frac{8}{9\pi}9π8​, 5\sqrt55​, ln⁡3\ln3ln3) unless asked for a decimal.
