Tonight's plan

Feel like you don't know the material yet? Do the crash course first (about 2½ hours): 10 short lessons, §2.8 to §4.1. Then come back here and start at step 2.

About four hours, in this order. The order matters: find out what you can't do first, then spend your time there instead of rereading things you already know.

  1. Formulas, 30 min. On a blank sheet, write every derivative rule from memory: power, product, quotient, chain, all 6 trig, exe^xex, bxb^xbx, ln⁡\lnln, log⁡b\log_blogb​, the inverse trig ones, sinh/cosh/tanh, and L(x)L(x)L(x). Check it against the cheat sheet. Then do one round of flashcards on All cards.
  2. Mock midterm, 55 min. Scroll down and start the 11-problem mock. Timer on, paper only, no peeking. This tells you exactly which sections need work.
  3. Fix the misses, 60 min. For every problem you missed, click its Review link. Open two or three of that section's problems and use Step through it like a debugger. Stop at the first line where your work and the walkthrough differ; that line is the gap.
  4. Drill weak spots, 45 min. In the problem generator, pick the sections you missed and do 5 fresh problems each. Make sure related rates (§3.9) and max/min (§4.1) get at least 3 each. Those are long, high-point problems.
  5. Quick quiz, 20 min. Quick quiz → Midterm 2 · everything (28 questions). Aim for 25+.
  6. Stop and sleep. 7+ hours. Sleep is when the formulas actually get stored; an extra hour of cramming at 2 a.m. costs more points than it earns.

Only have an hour? Do step 1 (20 min), then the quick quiz in Midterm 2 mode, then read the debugger walkthrough for one related-rates problem and one closed-interval problem.

Exam-day strategy

First pass

Do every problem you're sure of first. Mark the rest and come back. Don't spend 15 minutes stuck on one.

Show the setup

Write the rule you're using (“chain rule: outside′·inside′”). Partial credit lives in the setup.

Related rates

Picture → equation → differentiate in ttt → plug in. Units in the answer.

Max/min

Always make the table: critical numbers inside the interval plus both endpoints.

Check

Plug a simple xxx into your derivative and see if the slope makes sense. Check every sign when you distribute a minus.

No calculator

Leave answers exact (89π\frac{8}{9\pi}9π8​, 5\sqrt55​, ln⁡3\ln3ln3) unless asked for a decimal.
