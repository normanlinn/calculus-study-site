Study plan before the quiz

About 4 hours, in this order. Each block ends with a check: if you can do it without opening the solution, move on.

  1. 60 min · §4.3 shape of a graph. Read the 5 rules. Do #1, then #7 and #8 with the Sign-map walkthrough open. Then #3 on paper alone. Check: you can draw the number line, pick test points and write intervals, extremes and inflection points.
  2. 50 min · §4.4 l'Hospital. #1–#4 (0/0 and ∞/∞), #5–#7 (rewrite first), #8–#10 (take ln, answer e^L), #11 (check the form!). Check: #9 on paper alone.
  3. 10 min · break.
  4. 40 min · §4.5 curve sketching. Do #3 and #5 with the A–H checklist: domain, intercepts, symmetry, asymptotes, then the two sign maps. Check: you can find a slant asymptote by long division.
  5. 40 min · §4.7 optimization. #1, #4, #5, #7, #8. Always: objective, constraint, one variable, derivative = 0, justify. Check: #5 (box) on paper alone.
  6. 30 min · practice quiz (10 points), timed, closed notes. Tick what you got right and check the score bar.
  7. 10 min · read the Quiz 5 cheat sheet once, then rest. A clear head beats one more hour of cramming.

Right before the quiz (15 min): the quick quiz on Quiz 5, the Quiz 5 flashcards, and the 5 things to memorize below.
