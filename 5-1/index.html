Areas and Distances

Estimate the area under a curve with rectangles: left, right and midpoint sums.

0 / 5 problems mastered

Chop [a,b][a,b][a,b] into nnn strips of width Δx\Delta xΔx. Make each strip a rectangle whose height is fff at one point of the strip. Add the areas. More strips give a better estimate, and in the limit you get the exact area (§5.2).

Heights at the middle of each strip, xˉi=xi−1+xi2\bar x_i=\frac{x_{i-1}+x_i}{2}xˉi​=2xi−1​+xi​​. Usually the best of the three.

Over or under?

If fff is increasing, the left sum underestimates and the right sum overestimates. For a decreasing fff it's the reverse.

Distance

Distance ≈ Σ velocity × time step. Same idea with v(t)v(t)v(t) instead of f(x)f(x)f(x).
