When is f not differentiable?

Corner

Left slope ≠ right slope. Example: ∣x∣|x|∣x∣ at 0 (slopes −1 and +1).

Discontinuity

A jump or hole. Theorem 4: differentiable ⇒ continuous, so not continuous ⇒ not differentiable.

Vertical tangent

The slope blows up: ∣f′(x)∣→∞|f'(x)|\to\infty∣f′(x)∣→∞. Example: x3\sqrt[3]{x}3x​ at 0.

Careful

Continuous does not imply differentiable. ∣x∣|x|∣x∣ is continuous at 0 but has a corner.

Traps

Plugging in h = 0 too early

You always get 0/0 at first. Simplify until the hhh in the denominator cancels, then set h=0h=0h=0.

Forgetting to distribute the minus

Domain of f′ = domain of f

Not always. Check for division by zero in f′f'f′ (e.g. x\sqrt{x}x​ at 0).
